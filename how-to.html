How to

Transfer from Incheon International Airport (ICN) to Gimpo International Airport (GMP)

AREX Airport Railroad Recommended

After arriving at Incheon Airport, follow the signs for Airport Railroad (AREX). Go to the Transportation Center on B1 (basement level). Take the All-Stop Train toward Seoul Station. Get off at Gimpo International Airport Station. Follow the airport signs to the domestic terminal for your flight to Jeju.

Important: Take the AREX All-Stop Train, not the Express Train. The Express Train goes directly to Seoul Station and does not stop at Gimpo Airport.

Trains run approximately every 10–15 minutes. No transfers are required. This is the most economical option, especially for travelers with manageable luggage.

Travel time
About 40–45 minutes
Cost
About ₩4,000–₩5,000 ($3–4)

Airport Limousine Bus

After arriving at Incheon Airport, follow the signs for airport buses. Purchase a ticket at the airport bus ticket counter or designated kiosk. Board a bus that stops at Gimpo Airport. Get off at Gimpo Airport and follow the signs to the domestic terminal. Buses depart from the airport’s designated bus stops. No train transfers are necessary. A convenient option for travelers with large suitcases.

Note: Check the bus route and schedule before traveling, as not every airport limousine bus stops at Gimpo.

Travel time
About 40–60 minutes,
depending on traffic
Cost
Varies by bus route. About

Online Tickets

Taxi

Kakao T is Korea’s most widely used taxi app throughout Korea, including Jeju. It allows you to book regular taxis and larger vehicles. You can also use Uber.

Enter Incheon International Airport as your pickup location and Gimpo International Airport as your destination. Select your preferred taxi and confirm your ride. Follow the app’s instructions to find your designated pickup location at the airport.

Note: Kakao T might not be able to register your foreign cards. In that case, be prepared to pay in cash or with a physical card after the ride. For guests traveling with large suitcases or in groups, consider booking a larger vehicle.

Recommendation for wedding guests: Download both Kakao T and Uber before departure. Having both apps available gives you flexibility when arranging transportation, especially after a long international flight.

About 40–60 minutes,
depending on traffic
Cost
About ₩40,000–₩60,000
(approximately $30–$45), plus tolls.
