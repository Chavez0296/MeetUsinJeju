Travel

Flying

There is a few different options for flying to Jeju. Please compare prices & routes for your traveling preferences.
Flight connections from LAX to Jeju

Separate flights, with break Recommended

The most recommended way to fly to Jeju is buying two separate round trip tickets for LAX ↔ Incheon International Airport (ICN), and Gimpo International Airport (GMP) ↔ Jeju International Airport (CJU). This is the most cost-effective way to fly to Jeju.

We recommend spending a few days in mainland before taking another flight. If you’re traveling around Seoul, you can take Airport Railroad (AREX), subway, or taxi to Gimpo airport. If you’re traveling around Busan, you can fly from Gimhae International Airport (PUS).

Additionally, you can fly to any Asian city of your liking and fly to Jeju for the ceremony. Osaka, Tokyo, and Shanghai to name a few.

Straight to Jeju

You can also fly directly to Jeju without taking a break. You can either:

  1. Buy separate flights like above, but with a small layover in between
  2. Buy direct flights from Google Flights

However, you are responsible for transferring from INC to GMP even with direct flight tickets. Here is a guide to how to transfer between the airports.

How to

Transfer from Incheon International Airport (ICN) to Gimpo International Airport (GMP)

AREX Airport Railroad Recommended

After arriving at Incheon Airport, follow the signs for Airport Railroad (AREX). Go to the Transportation Center on B1 (basement level). Take the All-Stop Train toward Seoul Station. Get off at Gimpo International Airport Station. Follow the airport signs to the domestic terminal for your flight to Jeju.

Important: Take the AREX All-Stop Train, not the Express Train. The Express Train goes directly to Seoul Station and does not stop at Gimpo Airport.

Trains run approximately every 10–15 minutes. No transfers are required. This is the most economical option, especially for travelers with manageable luggage.

Travel time
About 40–45 minutes
Cost
About ₩4,000–₩5,000 ($3–4)

Airport Limousine Bus

After arriving at Incheon Airport, follow the signs for airport buses. Purchase a ticket at the airport bus ticket counter or designated kiosk. Board a bus that stops at Gimpo Airport. Get off at Gimpo Airport and follow the signs to the domestic terminal. Buses depart from the airport’s designated bus stops. No train transfers are necessary. A convenient option for travelers with large suitcases.

Note: Check the bus route and schedule before traveling, as not every airport limousine bus stops at Gimpo.

Online Tickets

Taxi

k.ride is a foreigner-focused taxi-hailing app that lets international travelers book rides in South Korea without needing a Korean phone number or a local bank card. It allows you to book regular taxis and larger vehicles. You can also use Uber.

Enter Incheon International Airport as your pickup location and Gimpo International Airport as your destination. Select your preferred taxi and confirm your ride. Follow the app’s instructions to find your designated pickup location at the airport.

Note: In rare occasions, the app might not be able to register your foreign card. In that case, be prepared to pay in cash or with a physical card after the ride. For guests traveling with large suitcases or in groups, consider booking a larger vehicle.

Recommendation for wedding guests: Download both k.ride and Uber before departure. Having both apps available gives you flexibility when arranging transportation, especially after a long international flight.

About 40–60 minutes,
depending on traffic

Getting Around

Car rental is mandatory in Jeju unlike mainland (public transportation is strongly recommended for cities like Seoul and Busan). Public transportation is very limited in Jeju. If you do not wish to rent a car, we suggest carpooling. We will reimburse up to $100 for each group that rents a car.

Car rental

First, make sure you get an International Driving Permit issued. AAA can issue one for you on the same day you walk in. Then, download the app or go to the website of either option below, and follow the instructions.

  • SK Rent a Car
  • LOTTE rent-a-car

Note

Google Map is not reliable in Korea. Download Naver Map or Kakao Map (of both!) for navigating throughout Korea, for both driving and public transportations.

Helpful Apps

Naver Map or Kakao Map

As noted above, please use these maps instead of Google Maps in Korea.

k.ride

k.ride is used to call taxis. In rare occasions, the app might not be able to register your foreign card. In that case, be prepared to pay with a physical card after the ride. You can also use Uber.

Papago

Papago is a translation app that allows taking or uploading pictures, which comes very handy when translating writings on signs or menus.

Baemin or Coupang Eats

These are two top food delivery apps in Korea. Food delivery in Korea is fast, cheap, and available almost anywhere.
